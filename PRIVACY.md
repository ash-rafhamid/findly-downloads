# Findly privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Findly is a Windows desktop app published by Plenixar. It searches filenames and folders on your computer.

## Information stored on your computer

Findly stores a local search index containing filenames, paths, file types, sizes, and modification dates. It also stores your search-location settings and exclusions. It does not index the contents of documents or analyze the contents of photos, audio, or videos.

The portable version stores its settings and index in the data folder beside Findly.exe. The Microsoft Store build uses per-user app data storage. The Store version is being prepared and is not yet published.

## Collection and sharing

Findly has no network code, account system, advertising, or analytics. The app does not upload your index, filenames, search queries, or settings to Plenixar or another service. Indexing and searching happen locally.

If you open a search result, Windows launches the selected file using its associated application. That application has its own behavior and privacy policy.

## Your choices

Use Search locations to choose folders or drives and exclusions. You can pause indexing or close Findly to stop its background work. You can remove the local index and settings when the app is closed; doing so does not delete the original files you searched.

## Distribution services and support

Download websites, Microsoft Store, and GitHub operate under their own privacy policies. Information you submit to those services is separate from Findly's local search data.

For support or privacy questions, use the [Findly issue tracker](https://github.com/ash-rafhamid/findly-downloads/issues). It is public, so do not post personal filenames, file indexes, or other private information.
